Your captain’s log

Four easy habits for calm, beautiful coastal clips.

1

Open on motion

A wave, a boat or a walking step. Movement in the first two seconds earns the watch.

2

Use soft light

Shoot near sunrise or sunset. Midday sun works best when the water is clear.

3

Keep it vertical

Frame for 9:16, keep the subject centered and leave room for captions.

4

Protect the place

Respect wildlife, reefs and local rules. Check drone laws before launching.
